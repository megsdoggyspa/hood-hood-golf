HHGI2 Format

Tournament Rules

4-Man Anti-Cheating Scramble

Every group is built for fairness, visibility, and competitive integrity from tee box to final scorecard.

HHGI2 Format

  • Each team has 4 players
  • Teams stay the same for entire event
  • Each 4-man team is split into two 2-man groups
  • Each 2-man group is paired with 2 players from a different team
  • No team ever plays alone
  • Every score is witnessed by non-teammates
  • Scores are recorded hole-by-hole
  • This protects integrity and reduces cheating
